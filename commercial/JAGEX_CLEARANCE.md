# Merch Desk Analytics — Jagex Policy Clarification Package

Jagex's Fan Content Policy reserves trademark rights and says commercial uses of Jagex Property outside the policy may require a separate agreement. Merch Desk Analytics is intentionally designed to avoid Jagex logos, copied artwork, account access and gameplay automation, but a written clarification is still useful before scaling.

## Questions to ask Jagex

1. May an independent paid market-analytics service describe itself as providing analytics "for Old School RuneScape players" if it uses no Jagex logo/artwork and clearly states it is not affiliated with Jagex?
2. May the service display plain-text item names and player-entered portfolio holdings for analytical purposes?
3. May the service consume the community RuneLite/Wiki real-time price API under that API project's rules and display derived market analytics to paying subscribers?
4. Is there any additional requirement Jagex would expect for a paid analytics/fan-site service that does not interact with the game client, automate gameplay, access accounts, or accept GP/items as payment?
5. Is a commercial licence needed for the proposed descriptive use, or would Jagex prefer specific wording/branding limitations?

## Draft inquiry

Subject: Policy clarification for independent Old School RuneScape market analytics service

Hello,

I am developing a small independent market-research service called Merch Desk Analytics for players who trade on the Old School RuneScape Grand Exchange.

The service is intended to analyze RuneLite/OSRS Wiki real-time market data, customer-supplied portfolio quantities/cost bases, public Jagex news, and publicly attributable developer comments. It would provide market research, portfolio analytics, catalyst tracking and conversion calculations to paying subscribers.

The service would not:
- use Jagex logos or present itself as official;
- copy the look of a Jagex website;
- access customer Jagex accounts;
- request passwords, bank PINs or authenticator credentials;
- interact with the game client or automate gameplay/trades;
- accept GP/items/accounts as payment;
- custody in-game wealth;
- provide account services, botting or RWT services.

The customer-facing brand would be Merch Desk Analytics. Old School RuneScape would only be referenced descriptively, with a prominent statement that the service is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Jagex.

Before scaling beyond a small beta, I would appreciate clarification on whether this proposed use requires a commercial licence or whether there are specific branding/content restrictions Jagex would want us to follow.

In particular, is it acceptable for the service to use plain-text game/item names and describe itself as market analytics for Old School RuneScape players, while avoiding Jagex artwork/logos and official-looking presentation?

Thank you.

Merch Desk Analytics
osrsmerchdesk@gmail.com

## Do not include in the inquiry

- customer data;
- internal passwords/secrets;
- speculative claims about Jagex mechanics;
- statements implying Jagex already approved the service;
- confidential business information you would not want treated as non-confidential.

Jagex's Fan Content Policy states that unsolicited commercial ideas sent without a confidentiality agreement may be treated as non-confidential, so keep the inquiry focused on permissions rather than proprietary implementation details.
