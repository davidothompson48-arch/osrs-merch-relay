# Merch Desk Analytics — Founding Beta Privacy Notice (Draft)

**Status:** Working beta draft. This is not legal advice and should be reviewed for the jurisdictions in which the service operates.

## Information collected during beta

The service may collect:
- contact email;
- Discord username;
- membership tier and paid-through date;
- payment confirmation/reference information;
- voluntarily submitted portfolio holdings, quantities, cost bases and watchlists;
- customer-provided risk/horizon preferences and portfolio constraints;
- customer support messages and product feedback.

## Information the service does not need

Do not send:
- Jagex/RuneScape passwords;
- bank PINs;
- authenticator codes;
- recovery codes;
- email passwords;
- browser cookies/session tokens;
- payment-card numbers;
- banking credentials;
- remote-access credentials.

Payment processing should occur through the selected payment provider rather than by Merch Desk Analytics collecting card/bank credentials itself.

## How information is used

Collected information may be used to:
- provide purchased access;
- maintain the customer's portfolio ledger;
- calculate P/L, concentration and market exposure;
- customize watchlists and research;
- administer renewals and support;
- improve trade-screening and service quality;
- prevent abuse and enforce service rules.

## Data minimization

Only retain information reasonably needed to provide the service, manage access, maintain business records and resolve customer support issues.

Do not store account credentials even if a customer sends them accidentally. Instruct the customer to change/revoke the exposed credential.

## Third parties

Beta operations may rely on third-party providers such as:
- Venmo/PayPal for payment processing;
- Discord for community/access delivery;
- GitHub and hosting providers for software infrastructure;
- the RuneLite-fed OSRS Wiki real-time price ecosystem for market-data inputs.

Those providers operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

## Security

Use reasonable security practices including unique passwords, multi-factor authentication, least-privilege access and separation of customer data from public repositories.

Never commit live customer portfolio records, payment references, private Discord information or secrets to a public GitHub repository.

## Retention

During beta, retain customer/accounting records only as long as reasonably needed for service delivery, bookkeeping, disputes and applicable legal obligations.

Create a formal retention schedule before scaling.

## Customer requests

Customers may contact `osrsmerchdesk@gmail.com` to request correction of inaccurate profile/portfolio information or ask about deletion of information that is not required to be retained for legal/accounting purposes.

## Children / age requirements

Before public launch, add jurisdiction-appropriate age/consent language based on legal review and the markets in which the service is offered.

## Changes

The privacy notice may change during beta as the service architecture evolves. Material changes should be communicated to active paying customers.
