# Merch Desk Analytics — Founding Beta Terms (Draft)

**Status:** Working draft for beta operations. This is not legal advice and should be reviewed by qualified counsel before material scale.

## 1. Service

Merch Desk Analytics provides independent market research, data interpretation, portfolio analytics, watchlists, catalyst monitoring and related informational content for players of Old School RuneScape.

The service does not:
- access customer game accounts;
- execute Grand Exchange trades;
- custody GP or in-game items;
- accept GP or in-game items as payment;
- provide botting, automation, account services or gameplay services;
- guarantee profits or market outcomes.

## 2. Eligibility

Customers must be legally able to purchase the service in their jurisdiction and must comply with applicable Jagex terms, game rules, and other third-party terms that apply to their own conduct.

## 3. Independent service

Merch Desk Analytics is an independent third-party service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by Jagex.

Old School RuneScape, RuneScape, Jagex and related marks remain the property of their respective owners.

## 4. Payments and access

Founding beta billing is manual.

Unless otherwise stated at purchase:
- Pro access is sold in 30-day periods.
- Private Desk access is sold in 30-day periods.
- Access begins when payment is confirmed and the customer is admitted.
- The customer's paid-through date is communicated at activation.
- No automatic renewal occurs during the manual beta unless separately implemented and expressly disclosed.

## 5. Cancellation and refunds

Customers may simply decline to renew future manual billing periods.

Refunds may be granted for duplicate payments, incorrect charges, failure to deliver purchased access, or other cases at the operator's discretion and as required by applicable law.

Market losses, missed fills, slippage, stale customer orders, changes in game content, or disagreement with a market call are not by themselves grounds for a refund.

Nothing in this section overrides rights that cannot legally be waived.

## 6. Market risk

All market analysis is probabilistic. Grand Exchange prices, liquidity, supply, demand and game mechanics may change quickly.

Customers remain solely responsible for deciding whether, when and how much to trade. Past performance, prior successful calls and simulated returns do not guarantee future outcomes.

## 7. Market data

The service may rely on third-party/public market data sources, including RuneLite-fed OSRS Wiki real-time pricing data. Data may be delayed, missing, malformed, stale or unavailable.

The service may refuse to issue a trade recommendation when data quality is insufficient.

## 8. Customer portfolio data

Customers may voluntarily provide item names, quantities, cost bases, watchlists and portfolio constraints for analysis.

Customers must not provide passwords, bank PINs, authenticator codes, recovery codes, session tokens or other account-access credentials. If credentials are sent accidentally, the customer should immediately change or revoke them through the relevant provider.

## 9. Prohibited use

Customers may not use the service to:
- coordinate market manipulation or artificial price movement;
- organize pump-and-dump activity;
- facilitate real-world trading of GP/items/accounts;
- facilitate botting, cheating, account services or other rule-breaking activity;
- impersonate Jagex or its staff;
- distribute malicious or fraudulent content.

Access may be removed for conduct that materially threatens the service, other members, or compliance obligations.

## 10. Conflicts and operator holdings

The service may analyze items also held by the operator. When relevant, the operator should disclose material exposure and must not use subscribers as exit liquidity or coordinate purchases to move thin markets.

## 11. Availability

Beta access may include interruptions, manual processes, experimental features and changing report formats. No continuous-availability guarantee is made during beta.

## 12. Intellectual property

The original analytics, writing, software, layouts, scoring systems and service materials created by Merch Desk Analytics remain owned by the service or their respective licensors.

Third-party names, trademarks, data and materials remain subject to their respective owners' rights and terms.

## 13. Limitation language for counsel review

Before launch at meaningful scale, counsel should add jurisdiction-appropriate provisions covering limitation of liability, disclaimers of warranties, indemnity where appropriate, governing law, dispute resolution, consumer rights, tax obligations and privacy/data-processing duties.
