FAQ

Clear boundaries.

Do you trade on my account?

No. The service never needs your Jagex login, bank PIN or account access. You control and execute every GE trade.

Can I pay with GP?

No. Access is sold only as a normal real-world software/research subscription. GP, items and accounts are never accepted as payment.

Is every trade profitable?

No. Market intelligence is probabilistic. The product is designed to improve decision quality, expose risk and reject weak trades—not guarantee outcomes.

Where do prices come from?

Current GE market data is sourced from the RuneLite-fed OSRS Wiki real-time pricing ecosystem, with transaction freshness and liquidity considered in every setup.
