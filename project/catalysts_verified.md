## The Fractured Archive / Raids 4

Official raid lock-in poll:
- Source: https://oldschool.runescape.com/polls/2026/1717
- Poll dates: 25 Jan 2026 - 2 Feb 2026.
- The Fractured Archive itself passed with 94.5% Yes (60,069 Yes / 3,472 No; 1,579 skipped).
- Jagex described the raid as aiming for a late-2026 release.
- This poll explicitly said raid rewards would be polled later.

Official rewards poll blog:
- Source: https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/the-fractured-archive---rewards-poll-blog?oldschool=1
- Published: 30 July 2026.
- Elemental Fragments: untradeable; consuming one for each element increases the max hit of that element's spells by 2.
- Rondache: shield with +2 Flat Armour and Magic Resist, stores mitigated-damage charges, instant retaliatory Shield Bash that rolls Crush, and Strength equivalent to Avernic but no accuracy bonuses. Jagex said it would monitor adverse effects on Avernic/ToB and could explore a combination route if needed.
- Zorya's Tome: two-handed Magic special-attack weapon; technically acts as a powered staff; 3-tick base attack, base max hit 18; special has +150% accuracy and +60% damage, and if it hits, the next three Tome attacks get the same buffs at 2-tick speed.
- Zeal: Piety override with +25% Attack, +28% Strength, +25% Defence; proposed requirements Piety, 84 Prayer, 75 Attack, 75 Strength.
- Ascension Crossbows: rapid-fire Heavy Ranged upgrade using regular, Diamond, or Onyx Ascension bolt variants; Ascension Bolts are made from Ascension Shards obtained from the raid or lobby activity. The blog does NOT state that existing Diamond/Onyx bolt tips are required ingredients.
- Breaker: Crush megarare; rolls damage twice by default, three times if Crush is the enemy's second-weakest Melee defence, and four times if Crush is weakest. Designed to dominate large Crush-weak targets. Jagex also proposed lowering Nex's Crush defence so Breaker becomes BiS there.
- Breaker charge requirement: Jagex explicitly said it will use Ascension Shards plus "a rune of some kind" and that the rune had not yet been decided in this blog. This confirms a broad rune catalyst, not Wrath specifically.
- Blog said Jagex hoped to open the rewards poll on August 3, with a possibility of delay.

### Reward poll result verification status

As of this project's 2026-08-18 verification pass, the official 2026 poll index surfaced polls through the July 21-August 2 Quarterly Survey but did not surface a Fractured Archive rewards result page, and targeted searches for Breaker/Zorya reward-poll results returned no official result.

A prior saved research note claimed "all six rewards passed." Preserve that statement only as UNVERIFIED LEGACY RESEARCH until an official Jagex/Old School poll result page is found. Do not treat the individual rewards as passed based solely on that prior note.

## Chambers of Xeric Summer Sweep-Up

Official source:
- https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/summer-sweep-up---agility--chambers-of-xeric-changes?oldschool=1
- Implemented 12 August 2026.

Unique weighting changes:
- Ancestral Hat: old 3/69 -> 4/60 regular, 4/56 CM (+53.3% relative regular, +64.3% CM).
- Ancestral Robe Bottoms: old 3/69 -> 4/60 regular, 4/56 CM (+53.3% regular, +64.3% CM).
- Ancestral Robe Top: old 3/69 -> 4/60 regular, 4/56 CM (+53.3% regular, +64.3% CM).
- Arcane Prayer Scroll: old 20/69 -> 14/60 regular, 12/56 CM (-19.5% relative regular, -26.1% CM).
- Dexterous Prayer Scroll: old 20/69 -> 14/60 regular, 12/56 CM (-19.5% relative regular, -26.1% CM).
- Jagex summarized the result as scrolls being about 20% less common in regular CoX and 26% less common in Challenge Mode.
- Jagex said these changes were large enough that exact market outcomes were difficult to predict and that it would monitor them rather than immediately tweak unless badly out of line.

Interpretation for desk:
- Scroll supply catalyst: CONFIRMED bullish supply-side change, but actual GE tape must confirm demand/price reaction.
- Ancestral supply catalyst: CONFIRMED bearish supply-side change, but future magic demand can offset it.

## Credibility rules for stored catalysts

- CONFIRMED = implemented game change or official passed poll/result.
- CONFIRMED PROPOSAL = official Jagex reward/design proposal, but pass/implementation not verified.
- STRONG EVIDENCE = multiple direct Jagex clues without final confirmation.
- PLAUSIBLE SPECULATION = logical inference with circumstantial support.
- WEAK SPECULATION = thin or indirect theory.
- RUMOR = unsupported community claim.

Always keep WHAT WE KNOW, WHAT WE THINK, and WHAT THE MARKET IS ACTUALLY DOING separate.
