# Elemental Magic / Surge Watch

Persistent watch added 2026-08-18.

Track the tradeable GE components and consumables tied to the high-end elemental Surge thesis:

- Harmonised orb — primary tradeable component of the untradeable Harmonised nightmare staff.
- Nightmare staff — second tradeable component required to assemble the Harmonised nightmare staff.
- Searing page — tradeable Tome of fire charge that adds the Sunfire minimum-hit effect to fire spells.
- Burnt page — input component for Searing pages.
- Sunfire rune — 100 are used with a Burnt page to create one Searing page and are also directly relevant to the fire-spell minimum-hit ecosystem.
- Tome of fire — off-hand enabling the fire-spell damage bonus and consuming page charges.
- Wrath rune — recurring rune consumed by Surge spells; core portfolio thesis.

Investment logic to monitor:

Elemental Fragments / stronger elemental weakness metas -> more competitive standard elemental spells -> greater Harmonised staff demand and Surge casting -> potential demand for Harmonised orb, Nightmare staff, Searing pages, Tome of fire inputs, and Wrath runes.

Do not assume all of these rise together. The Harmonised nightmare staff itself is untradeable; GE exposure is through the Harmonised orb and Nightmare staff. Searing pages are consumable and should be evaluated using fresh price/volume, Burnt page + Sunfire rune conversion economics, and actual PvM adoption. Every current market value must come only from prices.runescape.wiki/RuneLite data.
